# seereference.com Development Budget

## Purpose

This document defines the estimated funding allocation strategy required to develop seereference.com into an AI-assisted engineering ecosystem.

## Funding Target

Estimated Funding Requirement:

$300,000–$1,500,000 USD

## Budget Categories

## 1. AI and Software Development

Estimated Allocation:

30–40%

Purpose:

- AI system development
- Software engineering
- Platform development
- User interface development
- Data management systems

## 2. Prototype Development

Estimated Allocation:

20–30%

Purpose:

- Prototype design
- Engineering models
- Software prototypes
- Testing environments
- Product validation

## 3. Hardware and Testing

Estimated Allocation:

10–20%

Purpose:

- Computing equipment
- Prototype hardware
- Testing tools
- Development devices
- Experimental systems

## 4. Research and Development

Estimated Allocation:

10–15%

Purpose:

- Technology research
- AI research
- Engineering studies
- Technical evaluation
- Innovation exploration

## 5. Project Management

Estimated Allocation:

5–10%

Purpose:

- Planning activities
- Documentation
- Progress tracking
- Coordination
- Administrative requirements

## Funding Allocation Strategy

Initial funding priority:

1. Build technical foundation
2. Develop early prototypes
3. Validate concepts
4. Improve systems
5. Prepare future expansion

## Long-Term Investment Goal

Funding will support the transformation of seereference.com from a planning-stage project into a scalable AI-assisted engineering ecosystem.

## Financial Management Approach

seereference.com will use:

- Milestone-based spending
- Documented budget tracking
- Development reviews
- Resource prioritisation
- Continuous improvement
