# seereference.com Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This Risk Register identifies potential risks that may affect the development, launch, and growth of the seereference.com AI-assisted engineering ecosystem.

The purpose is to establish mitigation strategies and maintain project resilience.

---

# 2. Technical Risks

## Risk: AI Technology Complexity

Description:

Developing reliable AI systems requires continuous research, testing, and optimisation.

Impact:

High

Mitigation:

- Use modular architecture
- Begin with MVP features
- Continuously improve AI workflows
- Evaluate suitable AI technologies

---

## Risk: Infrastructure Scalability

Description:

Growing user demand may increase computing and hosting requirements.

Impact:

Medium

Mitigation:

- Use scalable cloud infrastructure
- Monitor system performance
- Optimise resource usage

---

# 3. Data Security Risks

## Risk: Protection of User Information

Description:

Knowledge management systems require strong privacy and security controls.

Impact:

High

Mitigation:

- Apply security best practices
- Protect user data
- Implement access controls
- Follow privacy requirements

---

# 4. Market Risks

## Risk: Strong Competition

Description:

The AI industry contains many established companies and platforms.

Impact:

High

Mitigation:

- Focus on specialised engineering and knowledge solutions
- Build unique user value
- Continuously improve products

---

# 5. Financial Risks

## Risk: Limited Funding

Description:

Insufficient funding may slow development.

Impact:

High

Mitigation:

- Prioritise MVP development
- Control operational costs
- Seek strategic investment partnerships

---

# 6. Operational Risks

## Risk: Development Delays

Description:

Technology projects may experience delays.

Impact:

Medium

Mitigation:

- Use milestone-based development
- Maintain clear priorities
- Review progress regularly

---

# 7. Compliance Risks

## Risk: Regulatory Requirements

Description:

AI systems may require compliance with changing regulations.

Impact:

Medium

Mitigation:

- Monitor regulations
- Design responsible AI practices
- Maintain transparency

---

# 8. Risk Management Approach

seereference.com will manage risks through:

- Regular project reviews
- Security-focused development
- User feedback
- Agile improvement cycles
