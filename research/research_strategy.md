# seereference.com Research Strategy

## Research Purpose

The research strategy defines the technical investigation required to develop seereference.com as an AI-assisted engineering ecosystem.

## Research Areas

### 1. Artificial Intelligence

Focus:

- AI-assisted engineering workflows
- Knowledge systems
- Intelligent design support
- Automation opportunities

### 2. Augmented Reality

Focus:

- AR visualization concepts
- Digital reference systems
- Future interaction methods

### 3. Engineering Systems

Focus:

- Product development workflows
- Prototype planning
- Technical documentation systems

### 4. Software Development

Focus:

- Platform architecture
- User interfaces
- Data management
- Secure development

## Research Process

Phase 1:
Technology evaluation

Phase 2:
Concept validation

Phase 3:
Prototype testing

Phase 4:
System improvement

## Research Outcome

Create a technical foundation for future seereference.com products and prototypes.
