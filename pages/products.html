Product Overview

seereference.com is focused on developing future AI-assisted engineering products and prototype systems that support design, visualization, and innovation.

Planned Product Areas

1. AI Engineering Assistant

Purpose:

An intelligent system designed to support engineering research, planning, design decisions, and technical workflows.

Potential Features:

  • AI-assisted problem solving
  • Engineering recommendations
  • Knowledge management
  • Design support

2. Digital Product Visualization

Purpose:

Tools for visualizing ideas, concepts, and product designs before physical development.

Potential Features:

  • Digital concept models
  • Design reviews
  • Interactive visualization
  • Future AR support

3. Prototype Development Platform

Purpose:

A structured environment for planning, creating, and improving technology prototypes.

Potential Features:

  • Prototype documentation
  • Development tracking
  • Testing records
  • Improvement workflows

4. AR Engineering Interface

Future development area:

Augmented reality tools that may allow users to interact with engineering concepts and digital references in real environments.

Development Approach

Products will be developed through:

  • Research
  • Engineering design
  • Prototype testing
  • User feedback
  • Continuous improvement

Future Goal

Create a connected ecosystem of AI-assisted engineering tools under the seereference.com platform.
